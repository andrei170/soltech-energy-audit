The Short Version
  • There is no clickable phone number and no enquiry form anywhere on your website. Somebody who finds you, likes what they see and wants a quote has nothing to do next. Everything else is secondary to that.
  • Your whole site is 430 words across five pages — homepage 109, about 96, contact 95, gallery 90, and a /cart page left over from the template you do not need.
  • Nothing is measured. No analytics, no tags, no call tracking. So you cannot compare what a Cosmic Solar job costs you against what one of your own would cost — which is the single most useful number in your business right now.
  • You are paying $400+ per job for work in San Diego, roughly eighty miles away, while telling us you want work closer to San Jacinto. There is no page on your site for San Jacinto, Hemet, Perris, Menifee, Moreno Valley or Temecula. Nothing local at all.
  • No service pages either — no install page, no battery page, no repair page. Google cannot show a page that does not exist.
  • You said you struggle to hire licensed people. There is no careers page. An installer looking for work in Riverside County cannot find out you are hiring.
  • What is already right: LocalBusiness and WebSite structured data are both present, the site works, and you own it. Nothing here is broken — almost none of it has been built yet, which makes it cheaper to fix than a bad site.
